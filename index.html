  • 12 Aug 2026 · 5 min read · PaymentsIdempotent payment webhooks in LaravelStripe, PayPal, Telebirr and QisstPay will retry. The second delivery must not post the payment again.
  • 3 Sep 2026 · 6 min read · ArchitectureTenant isolation on a multi-tenant SaaSThousands of organizations on one codebase only works if every query is scoped to the tenant that owns the row.
  • 18 Sep 2026 · 5 min read · LaravelMoving a Laravel monolith toward servicesSplit the paths teams already fight over. Leave the rest in the monolith until a boundary is real.
