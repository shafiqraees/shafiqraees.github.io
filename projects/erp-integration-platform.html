ERP integration platform

Middleware for enterprise systems. Connector framework, mapping engine, retry and dead-letter handling. Near-real-time sync across ERP, CRM and accounting, with new connectors shipping in days.

Shipped · 2017 – 2025 · Integration lead

Case study

  1. 01

    The challenge

    ERP, CRM and accounting each owned a copy of the same customer. A failed sync either vanished or blocked the next one.

  2. 02

    The approach

    I built a connector framework and a mapping engine on Laravel and Node.js. Kafka carries the events. Failures retry, then land on a dead-letter path. Elasticsearch supports search across the synced records.

  3. 03

    The outcome

    Sync stays near real time, and a new connector ships in days instead of a one-off integration project.

What shipped

  • Connector framework
  • Mapping engine
  • Retry and dead-letter handling
  • Near-real-time sync
